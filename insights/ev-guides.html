EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Essential Steps Before Buying an EV in India

  • ✓ Real-World Range Test: Always calculate real-world range as 70% to 75% of MIDC claimed range to account for AC usage and traffic conditions.
  • ✓ Home Electrical Audit: Verify your home sanctioned load capacity before purchasing a 7.2 kW Level 2 wallbox charger.
  • ✓ Warranty Terms: Inspect battery warranty clauses — OEM warranties cover capacity degradation if State of Health (SoH) drops below 70%.
SECTION 1 // THE BUYER CHECKLIST

Step-by-Step EV Evaluation Framework

1. Daily Commute vs Range Capacity

If your daily round-trip commute is 50 km, an EV with a real-world range of 250 km will only require charging once every 4 to 5 days.

2. Battery Warranty Terms & Cell Chemistry

LFP battery packs offer 3,000+ charge cycles and superior thermal safety. Ensure your warranty covers at least 8 years or 1,60,000 km.

3. Sanctioned Load & Discom Approvals

Submit a load extension request to your electricity provider if installing a 7.2 kW charger on a single-phase supply line.
