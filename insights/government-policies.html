EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Central & State Policy Incentives Overview

  • ✓ PM E-DRIVE Scheme: The central government's ₹10,900 crore initiative provides direct subsidies for electric two-wheelers, three-wheelers, e-buses, and public charging hubs.
  • ✓ GST Concession: EVs enjoy a concessionary 5% GST rate compared to 28% + compensation cess (up to 48%) levied on petrol and diesel cars.
  • ✓ State Purchase Subsidies: Over 20 states offer road tax waivers, registration fee waivers, and direct per-kWh purchase subsidies.
SECTION 1 // CENTRAL POLICY FRAMEWORK

PM E-DRIVE Scheme & Production-Linked Incentives (PLI)

India's electric mobility transition is supported by a robust multi-tiered policy framework designed to stimulate both consumer demand and domestic manufacturing capability.
