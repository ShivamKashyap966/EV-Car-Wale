Apartment Charging Guide

A comprehensive roadmap for setting up electric vehicle charging in Indian apartment complexes—from RWA permissions to safety checks.

10 MIN READ RESIDENTIAL INFRASTRUCTURE

As electric vehicles (EVs) become increasingly mainstream across India, one of the most critical challenges facing urban buyers is home charging—specifically for those living in multi-story apartment complexes and gated societies. Unlike independent homes where installing a charger is straightforward, apartment residents must navigate shared electrical grids, common-area parking structures, and Resident Welfare Association (RWA) approvals.

This comprehensive guide outlines everything you need to know to successfully set up an EV charging station in an Indian apartment society, drawing on legal frameworks, technical standards, and real-world installation best practices from leading operators like Bolt.Earth.

📌 Key Insight

Under the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) updated guidelines, electric vehicle charging is recognized as an essential service. RWAs cannot arbitrarily deny permission to install chargers on allotted parking spots, provided safety standards are met.

01

Check Rules & Permissions

Before running any wiring, obtaining formal approval from your RWA or society management committee is mandatory. The process generally requires a written request and a No Objection Certificate (NOC).

  • Right to Install: State electricity regulatory commissions (like in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Delhi) have set mandates requiring societies to facilitate EV charger setup.
  • NOC Processing: RWAs are legally required to review and respond to EV charging requests within a fixed period (typically 7–15 days).
  • Application Checklist: You must submit a formal application letter specifying your vehicle model, charger wattage, layout diagrams for cabling, and an electrician's safety certificate. You can find pre-formatted templates in our Apartment NOC Letter Template section.
02

Electrical Setup Basics

A safe, reliable charging setup requires careful planning of the building's electrical load:

  • Sanctioned Load Check: Check your household's sanctioned electricity load (found on your utility bill). A standard AC wallbox (7.2 kW) requires a load upgrade from your local DISCOM if your default sanctioned load is only 3–5 kW.
  • Dedicated Metering: Running a cable from your flat's domestic meter to your parking spot is the ideal choice for billing clarity. If the distance is too far, a separate sub-meter can be connected to the common utility panel, with consumption billed to your flat.
  • Earthing & Grounding: EV chargers draw sustained high current for hours. A dedicated earthing line with a resistance value of less than 2 ohms is essential to prevent shock hazards and avoid equipment faults.
  • Breakers & Safety Controls: Ensure your wiring setup includes a dedicated Miniature Circuit Breaker (MCB) and a Residual Current Device (RCD/RCCB) to instantly interrupt power in case of current leakage.
03

Charging Options in Apartments

Depending on your space, budget, and daily usage, choose one of these options:

🔌

1. 15A Socket

Basic slow charging using a weatherproof 15A socket. Adds 15–20 km of range per hour. Highly economical but takes 12–16 hours for a full charge.

⚡

2. AC Wallbox

A 7.2 kW or 11 kW wallbox charger. Adds 40–60 km of range per hour. Requires dedicated heavy cabling and load approval from the DISCOM.

🏢

3. Shared Chargers

RWA-partnered commercial chargers (e.g., Bolt.Earth Pro) in common slots. Uses app authentication and automated payments per unit.

Step-by-Step Installation Process

01

Site Feasibility Survey

Book a technical survey (Bolt.Earth provides certified surveys) to estimate cable run distance, assess the main meter room panel, and choose safe cable paths.

02

Obtain RWA NOC

Submit the formal NOC request with layout plans to the RWA committee and secure the signed certificate.

03

DISCOM Sanctioned Load Upgrade

Apply for a load upgrade or separate EV meter through your local DISCOM portal if your current household capacity is insufficient.

04

Cabling & Conduits

Lay heavy-duty armoured XLPE copper cables inside fire-retardant PVC/metal conduits. Run them along designated overhead cable trays in the basement.

05

Mounting & Grounding

Securely mount the charger on the wall/pillar at a height of 1.2 to 1.5 meters. Verify dedicated earthing setup is functioning.

06

Commissioning & Testing

Power on, verify voltage values, check leakage cutoff breakers, and connect the charger to the smart app (like Bolt.Earth) to configure access controls.

06

Safety Tips

Always prioritize these safety protocols to protect your home and vehicle:

  • IP Rating: Ensure all outdoor or basement chargers have at least an IP54 water and dust protection rating to handle washdowns and heavy rain.
  • Auto-shutoff: Verify the charger has built-in protection against over-voltage, under-voltage, short circuit, and high temperatures.
  • No Loose Wires: Avoid dragging loose cords across driveways where vehicles roll over them. Use holsters or hooks to secure cables.
  • Fire Safety: Mount a Class C or dry chemical powder (ABC) fire extinguisher near your charger location.
07

Common Mistakes

Avoid these common errors during the planning and installation phases:

  • Charging on standard 5A sockets: Standard household 5A sockets are not designed for sustained high current and can overheat, melt, or trigger electrical fires.
  • Skipping load assessment: Overloading your home grid without upgrading your sanctioned load will lead to frequent trippings and main breaker failures.
  • Poor earthing: Skipping a dedicated ground line or relying on weak shared grounds will prevent modern smart EV chargers from initializing.
  • Proceeding without written NOC: Verbal agreements from RWA members are not legally binding. Always obtain a formal written and stamped NOC.
08

Final Recommendations

- **Plan Ahead:** RWA NOC approvals and DISCOM load upgrades can take between 2 to 6 weeks. Start the paperwork as soon as you book your vehicle.

- **Rooftop Solar Integration:** If your apartment complex supports rooftop solar panels, investigate whether you can offset EV charging costs using solar energy credits.

- **Leverage Smart Scheduling:** Configure your charger to power your vehicle during off-peak hours (typically 11 PM to 6 AM) when grid demand is lowest and tariffs may be discounted.

💡 Key Takeaways: Apartment Charging
  • ✔ Start Paperwork Early: RWA and utility processes take 2–6 weeks. Don't wait for delivery day.
  • ✔ Use Armoured Cabling: Protect your wiring runs with high-quality XLPE armoured cables.
  • ✔ Verify Dedicated Earthing: Proper grounding is critical for safety and charger function.
